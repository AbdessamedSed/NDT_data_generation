Interpretation
--------------
The communication profiles show a stable and monotonic degradation:

C0 < C1 < C2

for RTT, measured jitter, synchronization delay, and synchronization
message loss.

All profiles use the same 2 Mbps link capacity. This avoids conflating
controlled delay/jitter degradation with bandwidth-induced queue
saturation.

ICMP packet loss and synchronization-message delivery are distinct
metrics. ICMP loss remained 0%, while effective synchronization delivery
decreased from 99.98% in C0 to 98.00% in C2.

AoI exhibits large inter-run variability and must not be interpreted as
a direct communication-link metric. It is affected by DT/ns-3 real-time
execution lag. DT/PT progression must therefore be analyzed separately.
